# Trade Wyse

## What is Trade Wyse?

Trade Wyse helps ordinary people create a professional online store for luxury products using AI — without coding, stocking products, or building a website from scratch.

It gives users a ready-made online business that looks modern, premium, and trustworthy from day one.

---

## What Trade Wyse Does

### 1. Creates Your Online Store Automatically
Trade Wyse builds a beautiful online storefront for you. You don’t need coding skills, design experience, or website setup knowledge. Everything is already prepared and professionally designed.

### 2. AI Helps You Create Better Product Listings
When you upload a product image, the AI helps by:
* Identifying the product details
* Researching similar products online
* Suggesting better pricing ideas
* Writing attractive product names and descriptions

This saves time and makes your store look more professional.

### 3. No Inventory Needed
You don’t need a warehouse or physical stock to get started. Trade Wyse is designed to let people focus on branding, marketing, and growing their business instead of dealing with complicated technical work.

### 4. Your Own Brand and Website
Every user gets their own store space. You can connect your own domain name, use a branded subdomain, and customize your brand identity. To customers, your store looks like a real independent business.

### 5. Referral Rewards Program
Trade Wyse also includes a referral system. People can invite others to join the platform and earn commissions for successful referrals. The platform provides tools to help them track referrals, monitor earnings, and manage performance.

### 6. Secure Payments
Payments are processed securely through Paystack. This helps ensure safe transactions, transparent payouts, and reliable payment handling.

## 🚀 Getting Started (Vercel)

1. **Connect Repository**: Import this project into your Vercel dashboard.
2. **Setup Environment Variables**: 
   * `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME`
   * `NEXT_PUBLIC_CLOUDINARY_UPLOAD_PRESET`=TradeWyse
   * `NEXT_PUBLIC_ROOT_DOMAIN`=your-app.vercel.app
   * `NEXT_PUBLIC_PAYSTACK_PUBLIC_KEY`
   * `GEMINI_API_KEY`
   * `PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY`
   * `RESEND_API_KEY`
   * `SERPAPI_API_KEY`
